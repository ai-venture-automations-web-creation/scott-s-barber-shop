Meet Scott

With over 15 years of barbering experience, Scott has built more than just a business—he's created a community cornerstone in Overland Park.

What started as a passion for the craft has evolved into Scott's Barber Shop, where every client becomes part of the family. Scott believes that a great haircut is just the beginning—it's the conversation, the atmosphere, and the genuine care that makes all the difference.

From classic cuts to modern styles, Scott's expertise ensures you'll leave looking sharp and feeling confident. When you're not in the chair, you'll find Scott involved in local community events, supporting Overland Park's growth and connection.
